AB 1482 (8.8% Cap)

limits annual rent increases to 5% base + local CPI (3.8% for Santa Cruz Metro).

AB 12 Deposit Limit

restricts security deposits to a maximum of 1 month rent starting July 2024.

SC Code 21.03 Just Cause

requires valid reasons to terminate tenancies of 12+ months and mandatory relocation for no-fault evictions.
