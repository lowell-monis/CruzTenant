SC Municipal Code Ch. 21.04

Rent Stabilization & 8.8% Cap

caps annual rent increases to 5% base + local CPI (3.8% for Santa Cruz Metro). Increases exceeding 10% require 90 days written notice.

SC Municipal Code Ch. 21.03

Just Cause Evictions & Relocation

requires legally valid reasons to terminate tenancies of 12+ months. Mandatory relocation assistance (2 months rent or $3,000 min) required for no-fault evictions.

California Civil Code § 1950.5 (AB 12)

Security Deposit Ceiling

restricts residential security deposits to a maximum of 1.0x monthly rent starting July 1, 2024.
